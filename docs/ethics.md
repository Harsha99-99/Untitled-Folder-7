# Ethical Research Guidelines

## Authorized Testing
- Only test devices you own.
- Obtain written permission for any third-party device.
- Document authorization before you start.

## Controlled Environment
- Use an isolated lab / controlled RF space.
- Prevent capture of bystanders' traffic.

## Data Handling
- Protect captured data; delete sensitive material after analysis.
- Anonymize findings before sharing.

## Responsible Disclosure
- Report real vulnerabilities to the vendor.
- Allow time for remediation before publishing.

## Legal Compliance
- Intercepting others' communications is illegal in most jurisdictions
  (e.g. wiretapping / computer-misuse statutes). Keep testing to your own
  devices and your own audio.

## Academic Integrity
- Document methods, cite sources, stay transparent.
